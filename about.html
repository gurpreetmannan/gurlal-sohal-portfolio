01 / Biography & Origins

The Power & Heart of “ਮਾਝੇ ਦੀ ਸ਼ਾਨ”

From the clay akhadas of Village Sohal, Tarn Taran to international stadium arenas across Canada, the United States, and Australia—with 16+ continuous years of elite active dominance, discover the life, discipline, and competitive spirit of Gurlal Singh, known worldwide as “ਕਬੱਡੀ ਦਾ Great Khali”.

Chapter 01 • Roots & Beginnings

Born in Village Sohal, Tarn Taran

Circle Style Kabaddi is intertwined with the soil and soul of Punjab. Gurlal Singh began his journey in Village Sohal, Tarn Taran, Punjab, India—a region renowned for athletic grit and martial tradition. Stepping into competitive circle style kabaddi in 2010 across Punjab village circuits (spanning 16+ competitive seasons), Gurlal developed an enduring passion for competitive wrestling and circle raiding, forging an imposing physical foundation rooted in discipline, perseverance, and fearless stamina.

Chapter 02 • The Majha Circuit & Aggressive Style

Forged in the Majha Circuit

The traditional Majha-region Kabaddi circuit is celebrated for its unforgiving speed, hard clay grounds, and formidable stopping chains. In this intensely competitive proving ground, Gurlal honed his trademark aggressive raiding style. Combining explosive forward drive with lightning-quick agility, razor-sharp baulk-line hand touches, and agile escapes (dubki and lion jump), he earned the affectionate moniker “ਕਬੱਡੀ ਦਾ Great Khali” for his towering height and unstoppable physical presence.

Chapter 03 • Punjab Championships & Scorpio-N SUV

Major Tournament Victories Across Punjab

Across Punjab's premier tournaments, Gurlal proved his status as a match-winning power raider. His crowning local achievement came at the prestigious Bir Baba Buddha Sahib Kabaddi Cup in Tarn Taran, where his tournament-winning raids earned him the grand vehicle prize—a brand-new Mahindra Scorpio-N SUV. This milestone was accompanied by a landmark performance at the 1st Frandipur Kabaddi Cup, where he scored an unblemished 18 out of 18 perfect raids (100% raid accuracy) to claim the Best Raider title and a prized traditional buffalo.

Chapter 04 • Global Circuit & Young Sports Club BC

International Playing Experience (Canada)

Elevating his game to the world stage, Gurlal has completed 15 international tours across Canada, the United States, Australia, Malaysia, and Europe. In Canada, he serves as the lead raider for Young Sports Club BC and Bal Incorporation. His performances in major Canadian tournaments—including the Royal King Kabaddi Cup, Surrey BC matches, and certified British Columbia season play—have captivated thousands of international fans, solidifying his reputation as a premier ambassador of Circle Style Kabaddi.

Village Sohal Roots & Tarn Taran Clay Akhadas

Began early wrestling and Circle Style Kabaddi training in Village Sohal, Tarn Taran, Punjab. Under the mentorship of local elders in the traditional mud akhadas, Gurlal forged exceptional lower-body power, balance, and quick baulk-line hand touches.

2016 – 2018 Majha Circuit Proving Grounds

The Majha Circuit Dominance & “ਕਬੱਡੀ ਦਾ Great Khali”

Rose through high-stakes rural Punjab tournaments across Amritsar, Gurdaspur, and Tarn Taran. Renowned for fearless raids against four-man stopping chains, Gurlal earned the iconic sporting monikers “ਮਾਝੇ ਦੀ ਸ਼ਾਨ” and “ਕਬੱਡੀ ਦਾ Great Khali”.

2019 – 2021 Historic 100% Accuracy

1st Frandipur Kabaddi Cup — 18/18 Perfect Raids

Delivered one of the most legendary individual performances in Punjab Kabaddi history, achieving an unblemished 18 out of 18 successful raids. Awarded the official Best Raider accolade along with a traditional prize dairy buffalo (ਮੱਝਾਂ).

Bir Baba Buddha Sahib Cup Victory & Mahindra Scorpio-N SUV

Triumphed at the celebrated Bir Baba Buddha Sahib Kabaddi Cup in Tarn Taran. As tournament champion and premier point-scorer, Gurlal was presented with the flagship prize: a brand-new Mahindra Scorpio-N SUV, symbolizing pinnacle sports recognition in Punjab.
